How it works

Every question goes through the same four steps.

RouteAn agent decides whether the question is about safety, maintenance, quality, or several of them.
SearchHybrid search finds the right sections by meaning and by exact part numbers and tags.
CheckSafety topics always pull in safety procedures, and prompt-injection attempts are refused.
AnswerYou get a short answer with numbered citations down to the section and page.

Built to be trusted on the floor

Safety is treated as a hard constraint, not a feature.

  • Safety firstQuestions about lockout, chemicals or guards always include a warning from the safety procedures.
  • No guessingIf the documents don't cover it, Ops Now says so and doesn't make up an answer.
  • Cited answersEvery answer links to the document, section and page it came from.
  • Your data stays putDocuments and the search index stay on your servers; the browser never calls the AI provider directly.
  • Full audit logEvery question, answer, cost and piece of feedback is logged for EHS and Quality review.
  • Tickets and memoryFollow-up questions stay in one ticket, so a supervisor can pick up where they left off.
